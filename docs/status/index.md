# Status

What you tell the people who depend on your services. In the app it is **Status** in the left menu (`g s`), with two tabs.

| Tab | What it does | Read |
|---|---|---|
| **Pages** | Public status pages, the services they show, and the incidents and maintenance you announce on them | [Status pages](../using/status-pages.md) |
| **Reports** | The availability (SLA) export of a period and the scheduled e-mail reports, with the history of their sends (administrators) | [Reports](../using/reports.md) |

A status page only shows what you put on it: the label you give each service, its state and its uptime. Monitor names, addresses and identifiers never leave the server.
