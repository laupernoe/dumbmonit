# Overview

The first entry of the left menu (`g o`): is everything fine, and what needs you. A night-sky banner says it in one sentence, five figures and the health river follow, then every monitor, worst first. Accounts limited by teams or clients have no Overview and start on **Monitors**.

The same board, full screen, is **Wall mode**: the screen icon at the foot of the left menu, with its own music.

| Where in the app | What it shows | Read |
|---|---|---|
| **Overview** | The banner, the five figures, the health river, the monitor table and the context panel | [Overview page](../using/overview.md) |
| **Wall mode** (foot of the left menu) | A black board for a screen in the room: the verdict, the clock, open alerts, who is on call, and the music | [Wall mode and music](../using/wall.md) |
| **Settings › Wall display** | What the wall shows, and the **Wall music** that plays on it | [Wall mode and music](../using/wall.md#music-on-the-wall) |
