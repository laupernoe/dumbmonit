Dispositivos que pode monitorizar

65 tipos de dispositivo em 10 famílias. Cada um é lido pela sua própria API, com uma conta só de leitura sempre que o fabricante a oferece. Escolha uma família para ver o detalhe.

As descrições dos dispositivos e os textos das regras de alerta estão em inglês, como no produto. As etiquetas da página seguem o seu idioma.

  • UPS with NUTUPS served by Network UPS Tools: on battery, low or worn-out battery, charge, runtime left, load and input voltage, for every UPS the server publishes.Servidores e hardware
  • Active DirectoryA Windows domain over LDAP, read-only: domain controllers and FSMO roles, privileged group members, password policy, and security findings such as Kerberos-roastable accounts, unconstrained delegation or an old krbtgt password.Servidores e hardware
  • SNMP deviceSwitches, routers, UPS, printers, access points: anything that answers SNMP.Equipamento de rede

  • FortiGateA Fortinet firewall through the FortiOS REST API: IPsec tunnels down, interfaces without link, HA out of sync, memory near conserve mode, FortiGuard licences expiring.Firewalls
  • Sophos FirewallA Sophos Firewall (SFOS) through its XML API: the link of every interface in a zone, and which IPsec connections are activated. Its live tunnel, HA and licence state are not in that API.Firewalls
  • Proxmox VENodes, virtual machines, containers, storage and backups of a Proxmox server or cluster.Hipervisores e clusters

  • VMware vSpherevCenter and ESXi hosts: hosts not responding or left in maintenance, VMs and their VMware Tools, datastores filling up, and the alarms vSphere raised.Hipervisores e clusters
  • XCP-ng / XenServerAn XCP-ng or XenServer pool through XAPI: hosts disabled or offline, VMs and their power state, storage repositories filling up, and a high-availability pool that can no longer survive the host failures it is set to tolerate.Hipervisores e clusters
  • Nutanix AHV (Prism Central)Nutanix clusters through the v4 API of Prism Central: hosts degraded or in maintenance, AHV VMs and their power state, storage containers filling up, and unresolved alerts by severity.Hipervisores e clusters
  • Kubernetes / k3sA cluster through its API: nodes not ready or under pressure, pods crash looping or stuck pending, workloads missing replicas, volume claims never bound.Hipervisores e clusters
  • Synology DSMVolumes, pools, disk health, temperature, Hyper Backup and Active Backup jobs, Synology Drive and Photos.NAS e cópias de segurança
  • TrueNASPool health, the failing disk, scrubs, snapshots, replication and TrueNAS's own alerts.NAS e cópias de segurança
  • UnraidAn Unraid server through its GraphQL API: array stopped, disks disabled or with errors, parity checks, cache pool usage, containers that should be running.NAS e cópias de segurança
  • Proxmox Backup ServerBackup freshness per machine, failed tasks, sync and verify jobs, datastore space.NAS e cópias de segurança
  • Veeam Backup & ReplicationA Veeam backup server through its REST API: jobs whose last run failed, failed sessions of the last 24 hours, repositories filling up, license expiry.NAS e cópias de segurança

  • TLS certificateA certificate is valid on any TLS port, with a warning well before it expires.Sites, certificados e verificações
  • NTP time serverA time server answers SNTP with a sane clock: offset, delay, stratum and leap indicator.Sites, certificados e verificações
  • WebSocket endpointCompletes the upgrade handshake, and can exchange a frame.Sites, certificados e verificações
  • Heartbeat (push)Your cron job or backup script calls DumbMonit; when it goes quiet, you are told.Sites, certificados e verificações
  • Domain expiryA registered domain, read from its registry over RDAP: days left before expiry, a hold that takes it out of the DNS, a redemption period, and its registrar.Sites, certificados e verificações
  • Website health & SEOKnow when a website breaks, goes blank, drops out of search engines or changes its title, description or canonical address, for one site or hundreds.Sites, certificados e verificações
  • Website changesBe told when the content of a page, or of a whole site, changes, with a before/after view of each change.Sites, certificados e verificações

  • NginxThe web server and reverse proxy: active and waiting connections, the accept/handled/request rates, and upstream health when the commercial NGINX Plus API is available.Servidores web e proxies reversos

  • Nginx Proxy ManagerThe proxy manager: hosts nginx refused to load, disabled hosts, and the certificates the hosts present, before Let's Encrypt renewal failures turn into expired sites.Servidores web e proxies reversos
